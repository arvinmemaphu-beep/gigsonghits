Privacy Policy — Gig Song Hits

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Gig Song Hits ("the App") is a personal songbook app for musicians to store chords and lyrics for their gigs. This policy explains what data the App handles.

Data We Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. We do not operate any servers or databases that store your information.

How Your Songs Are Stored

All songs, setlists, and folders you create are stored locally on your own device only, using your browser/device's local storage. This data:

  • Stays on your device and is never sent to us or any third party
  • Is not accessible by other users of the App
  • Will be lost if you uninstall the App or clear its app data, since there is no cloud backup

Permissions

The App does not request access to your camera, contacts, location, microphone, or any other sensitive device permissions.

Third-Party Services

The App may load a font from Google Fonts for display purposes. No personal data is shared with Google through this.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
